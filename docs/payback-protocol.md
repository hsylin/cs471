# Candidate payback follow-up

This study asks whether the exact native placement candidate has runtime value once installed, and whether that value pays for a real transition. It is a separate follow-up to the frozen TV-gate study. Old configurations, results, and reports remain historical evidence.

## Experimental unit and scope

Use pretrained Qwen3-30B-A3B layer 0 on the recorded two-H100 node. Capture real hidden states, original top-k expert IDs, and combining weights during teacher-forced causal prefill. Stable, transient, persistent, and switching timelines are **constructed replays** of those captured tokens; they are not observations of natural online temporal demand. Preserve exact logical routing and numerical inputs. Record source document/prompt identifiers, positions, token reuse, capture hashes, and replay indices. Performance claims are conditional on the chosen corpus, layer, topology, and execution path.

The resampling unit is a disjoint source-document block. These fixed, hand-selected books and CPython files are not a random sample of production workloads; disjointness does not establish population independence, particularly within the shared CPython project or authors. The block bootstrap describes variation across this prespecified finite corpus under a block-resampling assumption. Its intervals are not production-workload population confidence intervals. A block is reused across regimes, token batches, horizons, and arms, so these observations are dependent. Technical repetitions, capture/replay seeds, and several states from one block do not create new source blocks. Pilot and final source documents must be disjoint. Results are conditional on the measured node and allocation; a single allocation does not estimate variation across independent hardware days. If the required source blocks are unavailable, report descriptive results and insufficient evidence; do not rename repeats as blocks.

## Matched interventions

At a fixed decision state, generate one native candidate using only the observation prefix. Hold the candidate, source mapping and weights, future token sequence, hardware, memory budget, precision, and numerical checks fixed across three arms:

1. `KEEP`: retain the source placement for the measured future.
2. `PREINSTALLED`: install that exact candidate before timing, then measure its future execution.
3. `CHARGED`: start from the source state and charge the real transition and subsequent future execution.

Use 32 untimed observation inputs, then a common B3 prefix that can commit at measured step 63. Intercept the second opportunity after step 127; the future starts at step 128. Stable uses domain A throughout. Transient and persistent share a switch from A to B at step 120; transient returns to A at 136 while persistent remains B. Switching begins its alternating 16-step phases at 112, so its last observed window is B and its future starts A. The transient repeat period is fixed to 1,024 steps, beyond the recorded future, making it one transient within this experiment. A full tape has 672 inputs: 32 observation inputs, 128 prefix inputs, and 512 future inputs. This evaluates ongoing placement changes after an earlier native opportunity, rather than only correcting the initial map. Initial unused composed tapes had a 128-step recurrence; the switch to 1,024 was decided before payback timing and only the corrected tape hashes enter the frozen manifests.

There are no further placement updates during a branch. Candidate formation occurs before the common snapshot and is a sunk, excluded cost in **all three conditional branch timings**; record it separately. This diagnostic does not estimate a complete online policy's benefit. Any later policy timing must include observation, planning, decision, transfer, and execution. Installation must not silently become a host-side mapping rewrite: candidate weights and map must agree, and all arms must restore the same verified source state. Record source-map, source-weight, candidate-map, capture, and replay SHA-256 values. Use a common warm-up/cache protocol and verify outputs. Arm order must be balanced across blocks and repetitions. The arm permutation and allocation/run-block identifiers are retained in each record.

`KEEP − PREINSTALLED` is the benefit available from this candidate once resident; it is not an oracle over all placements. `CHARGED − PREINSTALLED` includes transition cost and interference under this timing protocol, and can also reflect residual cache-state differences. `KEEP − CHARGED` is net finite-horizon payback. A loss at a finite horizon does not rule out later payback. The three-arm comparison cannot establish production serving latency when the measured transport is replicated input plus allreduce; any actual token-dispatch implementation must be named explicitly in the frozen environment and records.

## Bounded design

The fixed design has four regimes, token batches 128 and 1024, and horizons 128 and 512. Each source block is a disjoint pair of one public-domain prose document and one PSF-licensed code document, capturing approximately 2,048 real tokens per domain. Sampling is without replacement within a replay step and may reuse tokens across steps. Pilot and final use two and eight disjoint document pairs respectively.

Freeze two candidate definitions before the first pilot: `native` is the unchanged native planner output; `one_copy` changes exactly one replica slot, selected solely by current-window peak-load reduction, with no future input or timing-based candidate search. The source-to-destination slot and deterministic tie rule are recorded by the runner. Run the native pilot first. Each candidate's initial screen has one repetition and 96 arm runs. If at least two changing states from distinct blocks have at least 1 ms charged payback at H512, confirm **all twelve** changing H512 states with repetitions 1 and 2, adding 72 arms. A robust pilot gate requires mean paired payback of at least 1 ms and at least two of three repeats positive in at least two states from distinct blocks. If native does not pass that gate, enable the previously frozen one-copy pilot using the same bounded 96-plus-72 design. No other action family or candidate search is permitted.

The final diagnostic includes **both** candidate families regardless of pilot outcomes, eight new source blocks and two balanced technical repetitions: 1,536 arm runs. Repetitions reduce timing noise; resampling still uses eight blocks, not sixteen independent natural sessions. Hardware correctness smoke tests are excluded from performance evidence. The one-copy comparison distinguishes an expensive native plan from the bounded smaller intervention without selecting a favorable family after seeing final timings.

Pilot may correct implementation failures, establish feasible capture/replay sizes, and fix timing/warm-up/order details. It may not be pooled into final estimates. Freeze the resolved final manifest and implementation/capture provenance before final timing. Any deviation must be recorded with its cause and timing; do not overwrite the original frozen manifest or reinterpret a changed experiment as the original confirmatory test.

## Outcomes and uncertainty

The primary horizon is 512 steps. Report paired ratios `PREINSTALLED/KEEP` (resident headroom), `CHARGED/KEEP` (net payback), and `CHARGED/PREINSTALLED` (transition penalty), plus paired seconds for interpretation. Within each block and cell, average the paired **log** runtime ratios across technical repetitions. Give cells equal weight when aggregating. Resample whole source blocks jointly across cells and arms with a fixed-seed percentile bootstrap; never bootstrap individual repetitions. Require all eight final source blocks for interval-based criteria, with the finite-corpus interpretation above. The 128-step horizon is a predeclared secondary descriptive horizon, not a replacement primary endpoint chosen after results.

Useful gain means a runtime ratio at most 0.95. For each candidate, the primary useful-gain aggregate gives equal weight to the six nonstable regime/batch cells. Stable regression uses `CHARGED/KEEP ≤ 1.02`, assessed for each stable aggregate and both token batches. A criterion passes when the interval's upper endpoint is at or below its limit, fails when the lower endpoint is above the limit, and is inconclusive otherwise. Failure of the 5% criterion does not imply slowdown. Use Bonferroni-adjusted percentile endpoints across the twelve changing cells (six per candidate), across the four stable cells, and across the two candidate aggregates respectively, separately for each contrast. These are explicitly defined endpoint families, not a global correction across every descriptive contrast. Secondary horizon intervals are descriptive.

The pilot-only 1 ms gate decides whether to develop at most one small online policy. It is a feasibility decision, not a significance test, a claim of 5% useful gain, or evidence that an online selector can identify beneficial states. Freeze any resulting policy, its baseline, its complete timing contract, and its final matrix before opening final blocks. Its primary useful-gain target remains 5%, with a 2% stable-regression guard. The three-arm final analysis does not select or adapt a policy. No reuse of viewed final outcomes as an allegedly unseen policy validation set is permitted.

## Stopping rules

If neither frozen pilot family demonstrates robust charged payback, stop new policy development and complete the final diagnostic for both families. If every primary-horizon nonstable cell rules out a 5% resident gain, report a negative boundary for that candidate family, not for all possible placements. If resident gain is supported but charged gain is ruled out, report the installation/transition boundary. If intervals remain inconclusive, complete the bounded study as inconclusive rather than adding blocks, horizons, margins, or candidate variants until a result passes. Final outcomes do not reopen the pilot-only controller decision.

Missing arms, incorrect outputs, mismatched source/candidate/replay hashes, incomplete horizons, and duplicate records invalidate the affected matched set. Incomplete declared matrices cannot produce a confirmatory pass. Report every exclusion and missing key; no imputed timing or result is permitted. All timing outcomes and the analysis are retained, including negative and inconclusive cells.
