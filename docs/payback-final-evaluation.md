**The completed experiments support a bounded negative result: these two candidate families offer small resident gains, but measured transition effects erase them on average.** All 1,536 held-out arms completed and passed the paired-data checks. On changing workloads at the primary 512-step horizon, the native candidate is 0.77% faster when preinstalled but 2.99% slower when its transition is charged. A legal single-copy replacement is 0.20% faster when preinstalled and 2.45% slower when charged. Both fail the frozen 5% useful-gain target and 2% stable-regression guard. No new controller was developed after the two pilot screens failed their prespecified continuation rule.

This is a scientifically sound conditional diagnostic and a suitable completed course project, with a bounded empirical contribution in its causal comparisons and reproducible artifact. Algorithmic novelty is low: it does not establish a superior balancing algorithm. The central improvement over the original proposal is measuring whether the available actions have enough value to justify a selector, using authentic router/activation records and actual native transfers and map commits.

**What explains the boundary.** Native moves change 49–88 slots and send 386,924,544–679,477,248 bytes. Single-copy moves change one slot and send exactly 9,437,184 bytes (9 MiB). The median measured H512 native transition call is 8.057 ms for the native proposal and 5.765 ms for single copy. These are complete synchronized native placement-transition times, including host preparation, transport, copies, map construction/commit and GPU completion. They are not network-transfer times or bandwidth estimates. Reducing bytes by this large factor did not produce a useful charged gain.

There are exceptions worth retaining. Native has three positive charged-payback observations among 128 H512 technical pairs, all from final block 07; single copy has none. Native switching/B1024 repays 2.619 ms and 2.541 ms in its two repetitions, yielding the only positive native state mean among 64 H512 states. The third positive observation, persistent/B1024, repays 0.394 ms in one repetition. These are held-out observations, not new pilot qualifiers or grounds to select a controller on the final set. They show why the conclusion must be candidate-, corpus- and horizon-specific.

The physical-count evidence also cautions against a balance proxy. Every candidate has positive current-window equal-sharing peak-load improvement. Actual future mean peak assignment count decreases in 30/64 native states and 41/64 single-copy states, while resident elapsed time improves in 36/64 and 55/64 state means. These descriptive counts do not establish a causal cost model; they show that predicted token balance, actual future balance and runtime are different quantities.

The final source states are mostly already adapted: the first B3 opportunity commits in 60/64 regime states, representing 15/16 distinct block/batch histories. At the target opportunity B3 would admit 36/64 native proposals and reject 28. All target commits are deliberately intercepted for the branches. Forced moves must therefore not be described as 64 ordinary migrations that B3 would execute.

**What was measured.** The experiment uses pretrained Qwen3-30B-A3B layer 0, 128 logical experts, top-8 routing, 160 physical slots, BF16 weights, and two H100 94 GB GPUs with NV6 connectivity. It captures the exact pinned embedding, attention, normalization and router computation for 40,960 teacher-forced prefill token records. Hidden states, logical expert IDs, native combining weights, source identities and original positions are retained. Four temporal regimes are constructed from prose/code banks: stable, a single transient, a persistent change, and 16-step window-aligned switching. Tokens are sampled without replacement within each batch and reused across steps. These are real captured numerical inputs with constructed temporal demand, not observed serving sessions.

The source state follows the actual B3 prefix through decision opportunity 63 and is intercepted at opportunity 127. The earlier opportunity can reject or commit. At the target, KEEP retains the source placement, PREINSTALLED installs the candidate before timing, and CHARGED includes native movement, normal map commit and synchronization inside timing. All three replay the same future with no later rebalancing. The two candidates are the unchanged native proposal and one legal single-slot replica replacement selected solely by current-window peak-token reduction. The latter preserves coverage of every logical expert and the fixed replica budget; no future timing chooses its slot.

Completed branch elapsed time includes Python replay, physical mapping, expert kernels, output all-reduce and final device completion. CHARGED additionally includes the complete synchronized native transition. Prefix, proposal formation, snapshot restoration, preflight and two conditioning inputs are common or diagnostic setup outside the timer, with excluded costs recorded. Therefore these are conditional candidate effects, not end-to-end online-policy effects. PREINSTALLED is a free-install diagnostic for this exact candidate, not an oracle over all placements. CHARGED minus PREINSTALLED includes cache/placement-transition effects as well as native transfer and commit; it is not pure network time.

The implemented path is pinned vLLM `db9527a46873454610df6dbedf79a36d6bf1a7f6`, synchronous `torch_nccl` weight transport, replicated inputs and output all-reduce. It is not request-sharded all-to-all inference. Execution logs show that `E=80,N=768,device_name=NVIDIA_H100.json` is absent, so all arms use the same native default fused-MoE configuration. The result is conditional on that configuration, not a bound on optimally tuned H100 kernels.

**The decision was frozen before final timing.** Native and single-copy screening each completed 96 arms, with no missing or invalid triples. Neither had a changing H512 state with at least 1 ms charged payback; the rule required qualifying states from two distinct pilot blocks. All sixteen H512 observations per family, including stable cases, had negative charged payback. Confirmation and controller development therefore stopped, as specified before measurement. The 24-arm correctness smoke is excluded from performance evidence. Pilot observations are not pooled with final estimates.

H512 is primary and H128 secondary. A useful gain requires a ratio at most 0.95; stable regression must remain at most 1.02 for both batch cells and the stable aggregate. A criterion passes only when its adjusted interval's upper endpoint meets the margin, fails when its lower endpoint exceeds the margin, and otherwise remains inconclusive. A failed 5% gain criterion alone is not evidence of slowdown.

Paired log ratios are averaged over two technical repetitions within each source block and cell. The changing aggregate gives equal weight to its six regime/batch cells. The frozen bootstrap resamples eight whole source-document blocks, with 20,000 draws and seed 471. Bonferroni-adjusted endpoints cover twelve changing cells, four stable cells and two candidate aggregates, separately for each contrast. These are finite-corpus resampling intervals under the stated assumption. The hand-selected books and files from one Python project are not a random production population, and one allocation cannot estimate hardware-day variation.

**Interpretation and prior work.** The contribution is experimental discrimination between candidate execution value and transition cost. It is not a new placement or forecasting algorithm. METRO and TEMPO motivate attention to physical expert activation, dispatch and tile work; METRO also changes transport, so its reported gains do not isolate dispatch alone. Gimbal and FreeBalance already incorporate movement or overlap costs into more extensive systems. The reviewed METRO, TEMPO and FreeBalance versions are preprints; Gimbal is an arXiv manuscript with an author-reported ATC 2026 designation. Their full-system reported gains do not predict gains for this isolated layer. The source review and inspected open-source snapshots support the choice of diagnostic, not the experimental result itself. [METRO](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2512.09277), [TEMPO](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2608.13057), [Gimbal](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2606.15177), [FreeBalance](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2608.14205).

The earlier TV study remains separate: its B4/B3/B5 comparisons were already completed on controlled synthetic routing, and the TV gate did not meet its useful-gain criterion. This follow-up does not remeasure TV's selection quality on authentic routes. Instead, it tests the prerequisite that the two frozen candidate families offer enough measurable benefit to justify another selector. Lower imbalance and fewer bytes do not substitute for charged elapsed-time gain. The raw-input audit also verifies identical transient/persistent observations through the decision: a historical statistic cannot distinguish those futures at that point without additional information. That is a property of the constructed intervention, not a claim that natural workload changes are universally unpredictable.

The five consequential limits are ranked by their effect on interpretation:

1. **Execution scope, necessary claim limit.** One layer, one two-GPU topology, replicated-input/allreduce dispatch and default kernel tuning cannot establish full-model or optimized-serving performance. Keep that scope in every headline; changing the transport or tuning the kernel is a separately frozen extension.
2. **Workload support, necessary claim limit.** Authentic activations do not make the constructed prose/code schedules natural sessions. Eight fixed document pairs provide limited corpus variation. More representative natural traces and another allocation would strengthen external validity but are optional extensions to this bounded course project.
3. **Candidate restriction, necessary claim limit.** Native token-balance placement and one peak-load-selected slot change do not exhaust useful placements. A negative result cannot rule out an objective based on actual physical work, a different dispatch rule, overlap, or a longer amortization horizon. Searching those after seeing the held-out outcomes would require new validation data.
4. **Timing decomposition, necessary interpretation.** The branch timer excludes common decision preparation, and preinstalled/charged cache states differ by design. Transition cost cannot be assigned to network bandwidth, CPU work or cache disruption from this experiment alone. A complete online policy would have to charge observation and planning as well.
5. **Validation reach, necessary disclosure.** Full mapping and weight checks and independent logical-to-physical reconstruction are stronger than token-count summaries, but FP32 numerical references cover four tokens at each of two boundary inputs, not every timed output. Independent analysis code within this project is not independent laboratory reproduction.

These limits do not require enlarging the completed course project. They require a bounded conclusion and reusable evidence. A further proposal should first demonstrate profitable charged actions under a newly frozen, justified candidate/execution configuration before adding a predictor or admission rule. Full-model serving integration remains optional.
