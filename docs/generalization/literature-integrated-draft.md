# Generalization evidence synthesis

The literature supports a narrow next claim: whether the frozen combination of no-op suppression and resident replica dispatch retains its incremental response benefit on new content and externally derived arrival calendars through the same model layer. It does not support presenting activated-expert minimization, movement avoidance or sparse execution as new principles. The 50-paper matrix spans 18 MoE systems papers, 17 serving papers and 15 architecture or evaluation papers. Their methods inform distinct parts of this test; their heterogeneous speedups are not replications that can be pooled into an expected improvement.

## Mechanism and novelty

Expert execution cost is not determined by token imbalance alone. MegaScale-Infer includes a cold-expert cost floor, while Tutel adapts execution and communication to shape and parallelism. MegaBlocks exposes the cost of padding small expert groups, but its isolated-kernel experiment excludes sparse-metadata construction. Together these results justify measuring active copies, token counts and padded work alongside completed latency. They do not establish that minimizing any one proxy minimizes runtime. MegaScale-Infer's expert allocation fractions are also different from a binary charge for each active physical replica. [MegaScale-Infer](https://doi.org/10.1145/3718958.3750506), [Tutel](https://proceedings.mlsys.org/paper_files/paper/2023/hash/5616d34cf8ff73942cfd5aa922842556-Abstract-mlsys2023.html), [MegaBlocks](https://proceedings.mlsys.org/paper_files/paper/2023/hash/5a54f79333768effe7e8927bcccffe40-Abstract-mlsys2023.html).

The closest objective-level overlap is explicit in the supplementary preprints. METRO assigns active logical experts to eligible resident locations to reduce activated-expert load, while also changing token transport. TEMPO combines activation and tile-rounded compute costs and reports settings in which adaptation brings little benefit. Gimbal includes movement penalties; FreeBalance constrains swaps by transfer cost and available overlap. These works prevent a broad novelty claim for activation-aware dispatch or migration restraint. The current contribution is more local: a concrete exact-routing implementation, charged online costs, a matched shortcut comparator and an independently checkable test of its effect. The four manuscripts remain outside the conference-paper count, and their incomplete artifact availability limits reproducibility claims. [METRO v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.09277v1), [TEMPO v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.13057v2), [Gimbal v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.15177v1), [FreeBalance v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.14205v1).

Reducing apparent work can still worsen hardware execution. SparseTIR sometimes adds padding to improve tensorization and balance; SonicMoE separates exact IO/scheduling optimizations from token rounding that changes assignments. SmartMoE likewise distinguishes placement improvement from online search cost. These comparisons favor keeping the checkpoint, logical top-k, combining weights, residency map and kernel configuration fixed while varying only the implemented physical dispatch and unchanged-map shortcut. They do not justify replacing authentic routes with balanced synthetic gates or treating a tile count as a memory-bandwidth measurement. [SparseTIR](https://doi.org/10.1145/3582016.3582047), [SonicMoE](https://proceedings.iclr.cc/paper_files/paper/2026/hash/6ed5bf446f59e2c6646d23058c86424b-Abstract-Conference.html), [SmartMoE](https://www.usenix.org/conference/atc23/presentation/zhai).

The three-arm design separates a useful incremental contrast but does not estimate a factorial interaction. Combined versus shortcut measures the dispatcher's addition to the shortcut path; combined versus native measures the overall package. A fast combined/native result alone cannot show that dispatch helped. All selector, mapping, monitoring, rank-agreement and coordinator work belongs in those timed comparisons. Lina's separate unprofiled timing and profiler runs, and PIT's distinction between isolated sparse kernels and dynamic indexing costs, support keeping mechanism diagnostics outside primary performance runs. [Lina](https://www.usenix.org/conference/atc23/presentation/li-jiamin), [PIT](https://doi.org/10.1145/3600006.3613139).

## Workloads and response

Fresh content and fresh arrival times address different limitations. Several influential serving evaluations combine natural prompt lengths with Poisson or Gamma arrivals; others reconstruct arrivals from aggregated workload traces. These choices create useful controls but do not preserve the original joint distribution of prompt semantics, expert demand and arrival time. Public Azure data provide timestamp and token-count evidence without corresponding prompt text. Pairing those timestamps with new mathematical, code and conversation captures therefore creates trace-derived layer jobs, not recovered customer requests. [PagedAttention](https://doi.org/10.1145/3600006.3613165), [AlpaServe](https://www.usenix.org/conference/osdi23/presentation/li-zhouhan), [Splitwise](https://doi.org/10.1109/ISCA59077.2024.00019), [DynamoLLM](https://doi.org/10.1109/HPCA61900.2025.00102).

The appropriate workload record begins before timing. It includes selected source documents, captured token positions, prefix-deduplication rules, original timestamp rows, calendar scaling and the exact grouped inputs. WildChat broadens semantic coverage, but its collection process does not make every sampled conversation representative of production users. The same applies to code and mathematical strata. Teacher-forced hidden states and router choices are authentic model observations; their assembly into fixed 128-token jobs remains constructed. Source-disjoint content tests a stronger limitation than additional permutations of an old bank, without extending the claim beyond the pinned layer. [WildChat](https://proceedings.iclr.cc/paper_files/paper/2024/hash/9421261e06f1a63a352b068f1ac90609-Abstract-Conference.html).

Response latency must retain the queue generated by the offered schedule. MLPerf distinguishes server, offline and single-stream scenarios, and DistServe separates constraints on first-token and subsequent-token latency. These metrics cannot be replaced by a faster GEMM or a shorter active-service interval. The present response begins at an immutable scheduled release and ends after both ranks' completion is acknowledged; every scheduled job remains in the accounting. Its finite-run p95 is a descriptive tail endpoint for that replay, not a steady-state production-tail guarantee or full-model TTFT. [MLPerf Inference](https://doi.org/10.1109/ISCA45697.2020.00045), [DistServe](https://www.usenix.org/conference/osdi24/presentation/zhong-yinmin).

## Attribution and uncertainty

Precision within a setup cannot repair a biased setup or an inappropriate inference unit. Mytkowicz and colleagues demonstrate that setup choices can reverse an optimization conclusion, while Kalibera and Jones distinguish repetition at different experimental levels. Their lesson here is to retain entire content bundles, including both arrival types and both rate conditions, when resampling. The frozen content-stratified bootstrap is conditional on the selected external trace material and one allocation. It does not estimate separate content, service, calendar-day and hardware variance components. Deleting a shared actual date jointly across services exposes temporal sensitivity without manufacturing more independent days from hundreds of jobs. [Mytkowicz et al.](https://doi.org/10.1145/1508244.1508275), [Kalibera and Jones](https://doi.org/10.1145/2464157.2464160).

The study should preserve its declared primary contrast, dataset/rate safeguards and gated native-baseline comparison regardless of their outcomes. The 5% usefulness and 2% regression margins are carried-forward engineering choices, not universal constants inferred from the reviewed papers. All six treatment orders should be balanced within each rate so that overall balance does not conceal a rate-specific execution-order confound. Per-group and per-date results remain useful explanations of failure, but selecting a favorable subgroup after measurement would require a new confirmatory study.

Project documentation clarifies the remaining boundary. NVIDIA's Wide-EP and vLLM's large-scale examples measure broader stacks; DeepEP supplies transport interfaces with their own integration contract. Those sources support separating local dispatch from the costs of obtaining global routes and moving tokens. They do not establish compatibility or speedup for a replacement transport here. The present test retains replicated inputs and output allreduce, with no interrequest batching, autoregressive decode or full-model scheduling. The [supplementary source inventory](supplementary-sources.md) records these exact primary URLs and scope limits separately from the conference evidence.

The defensible result, favorable or unfavorable, concerns the frozen implementation on the prespecified new content and trace-derived calendars at one Qwen layer, batch size, residency budget and two-H100 allocation. Novel models, layers, GPU families, kernels and token-transport systems are boundaries of the claim rather than additional tasks. The [ten-work appendix](closest-work-appendix.md) provides the closest implementation comparisons, while the [eight-paper extraction audit](independent-cross-paper-audit.md) checks selected high-risk literature claims. Neither document represents external experimental reproduction or independent auditing of all 50 papers.
