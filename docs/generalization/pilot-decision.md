# Pilot decision before final timing

All 72 frozen pilot treatment trials completed on allocation 4872248. The production analyzer accepts every trial and all 24 matched triplets. Independent execution and statistics audits are archived separately; final treatment starts only after they pass.

The unchanged planning rule selects six content groups per dataset: the sample SD of the six four-condition bundle log ratios is 0.04676959, giving a projected log half-width of 0.02834193, below 0.03. This is a limited precision heuristic, not a guarantee that the primary or seven guards will pass. The final selection is by source-ID hash, without final routing or timing optimization. There will be 216 final treatment trials, 72 pilot trials and 24 separately labeled diagnostics, totaling 312. Native-only calibrations are additional preparation.

No algorithm or placement change is selected. All six dataset/rate pilot aggregate combined/shortcut response ratios are below one, and the data do not identify a reproducible negative regime that would justify a fallback. MBPP has a smaller descriptive benefit than WildChat. That difference motivates explanation using the frozen diagnostics, not a new pilot-fitted threshold. Apparent pilot improvement does not establish the final hypothesis.

The fixed map, replica budget, B128 shape, checkpoint, dispatcher, shortcut, primary endpoint, seven guards, date-deletion rule and conditional inference remain unchanged. The common absolute arrival rate stays derived from the six eligible pilot native calibrations. Per-content calibrated rates will use the selected final groups' native calibrations on the same allocation. No final treatment outcome has been measured at this decision.
