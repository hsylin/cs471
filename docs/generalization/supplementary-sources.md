# Supplementary implementation evidence

The project and company sources support an implementation boundary: expert dispatch, communication, placement and serving orchestration are separate costs, and a gain in one does not establish a gain for the complete service. These sources complement the 50 conference papers; they contribute zero papers to that count. The companion JSON records source URLs, raw-byte hashes, retrieval checks and exclusions.

## Project and company sources

[NVIDIA's Wide-EP article](https://developer.nvidia.com/blog/scaling-large-moe-models-with-wide-expert-parallelism-on-nvl72-rack-scale-systems/) relates expert-weight traffic to communication topology. Its Figure 5 compares EP32 with EP8 on GB200 NVL72 with disaggregated serving and multi-token prediction. That author-reported rack-scale result does not establish the effect of resident replica dispatch on two H100s. The relevant transfer is to measure the complete critical path and preserve the hardware context. The AI-generated summary and unrelated article teasers were excluded from extraction.

[The vLLM large-scale serving article](https://vllm.ai/blog/2025-12-17-large-scale-serving) describes sliding-window EPLB, communication overlap and a collection of kernel and serving changes. Its H200 throughput result is a combined-stack observation. It does not isolate the no-op shortcut or the resident dispatcher. The article helps identify costs absent from the present replicated-input layer replay, particularly cross-rank token transport and prefill/decode scheduling.

[DeepEP at commit `f6aa38f`](https://github.com/deepseek-ai/DeepEP/blob/f6aa38fe06cd4a0489e7730365d4e0114970a2f3/README.md) documents dispatch/combine communication and expert-weight exchange; replication planning and rerouting remain caller responsibilities. The pinned README requires Hopper or newer GPUs, CUDA 13.1+, PyTorch 2.10+ and NCCL 2.32.3+. Documented Hopper support is not a tested adapter for this frozen environment. Its profiling flags can deliberately return placeholder internal timings, which reinforces the need to distinguish diagnostics from performance measurements. No DeepEP installation or execution was performed.

## Literature exclusions

The eight supplementary MoE records are not eight preprints. METRO, Gimbal, TEMPO and FreeBalance are retained as preprints, with publication and artifact limits stated individually. Libra, Toward Efficient Inference for Mixture of Experts, FasterMoE and ScheMoE have verified conference identities but lacked complete retrieved methods/evaluation text in this review. They remain outside the 50-paper evidence matrix. Repository availability does not turn an incomplete paper extraction into a completed conference-paper review. Exact publication links and reading status appear in [the supplementary inventory](supplementary-sources.json).

METRO and TEMPO are especially important novelty constraints because they directly examine activated experts and nonlinear expert execution cost. Gimbal and FreeBalance already restrain costly reconfiguration. Their relevance does not remove their preprint status or supply an independent reproduction. The [ten-work appendix](closest-work-appendix.md) distinguishes paper mechanisms, inspected implementation points and ideas that the current fixed-layer experiment cannot test.

## Retrieval and cross-checking

The initial retrieval log recorded three Python urllib certificate failures. The source files were subsequently obtained, their content and SHA-256 values were checked, and a new normal-TLS curl request returned HTTP 200 for each exact URL. The corrected log preserves the failed attempts rather than erasing them. The pinned DeepEP and vLLM rechecks match the cached bytes. NVIDIA's HTML bytes differ between requests, but the extracted visible text is identical after excluding scripts/styles and normalizing whitespace. Both byte versions and headers are retained, and the inventory identifies the original inspected snapshot. A web-reader timeout for NVIDIA did not invalidate the separately successful direct retrieval.

Its [machine-readable record](independent-cross-paper-audit.json) preserves the source checksums and corrections. This is a targeted same-team second-reviewer check of eight papers, not an independent audit of all 50 and not reproduction of any reported experiment.
