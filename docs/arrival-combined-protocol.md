# Resident dispatch with unchanged-map skipping

We will test whether the existing current-batch resident dispatcher provides additional response-time benefit when combined with the already validated unchanged-map shortcut. The incremental comparison will be combined versus shortcut alone. Native SKIP will be a contemporaneous third arm. Neither existing optimization will be retuned. The three-arm study estimates their incremental policy effect; it cannot establish an interaction or synergy without a fourth online-only arm, which is outside this bounded experiment.

The system will retain one Qwen3-30B-A3B BF16 layer, 128 logical experts, top-eight routing, 160 fixed resident slots including 32 redundant copies, and the pinned synchronous vLLM implementation. The earlier source and resident manifest remain immutable. All phases will execute sequentially inside reservation 4872248 on kh014, using the same H100 94 GB pair and NV6 connection: GPU-204281f9-9c87-3c25-adfb-9edd6c972ec3 and GPU-7d5c9009-305b-9341-957f-1deb96611ed5. Host, physical UUIDs and actual rank assignments will be verified. The held reservation ends on 11 November 2026 at 16:45:31 Zurich time and will remain active after these experiments until that end time, as previously requested.

The arms will be named native, shortcut and combined. Native will use the unwrapped native SKIP path and native physical dispatch. Shortcut will use that same dispatcher and the existing rank-agreed unchanged-map movement/commit shortcut. Combined will additionally install the frozen current-batch resident-copy dispatcher. Its three GPU kernels, output allocation and launch costs will remain inside service timing. It chooses one resident home for each duplicated expert from the current batch only; no future routes or precomputed assignments will be used to execute the treatment. The CPU oracle will remain an untimed verifier. Both optimized arms will charge the unchanged-map equality check and Gloo agreement. The fixed resident dispatcher does not support changed placement; no migration-performance claim will be made for the combination.

Native planning, 16-job observation windows, 64-job planning cadence, terminal handling, completion synchronization and CPU command/ACK protocol will remain unchanged. Native and shortcut will have identical physical routes. Combined may have different physical-slot counts, but they must collapse to exactly the same logical expert demand. All arms must therefore retain the same logical planner inputs, proposed and committed maps, effective placement and source weights. The existing SKIP policy suppresses all actual migration. The terminal job suppresses a default proposal while still executing the unchanged-map path: each 256-job trial has three proposals and four movement/commit pairs.

For each immutable scheduled arrival a, coordinator admission e, command start s and acknowledged GPU completion f, response will be f−a, queue waiting s−a and charged service f−s. The absolute-clock FCFS wrapper will remain unchanged, with one job in flight and all 256 jobs retained through completion. A job is a preassembled batch of 128 or 1,024 captured token inputs through one layer, not a full-model request. Completed observed elapsed time will include the offered horizon, all drain and observation overshoot. The identical input-preparation, warm-up, conditioning and correctness procedures will be recorded as excluded setup. Timing will contain all dispatcher selection, planner, agreement, communication, synchronization and per-job coordinator overhead. GPU probes and profilers will not run concurrently with measurement.

We will first run a correctness pilot using the previously used opt-pilot-01/domain1 payloads and old constant/burst-eight arrival calendars at nominal load 0.85. Two batches, two calendars, three arms and two reversed repetitions give 24 trials. Before timing each batch, an untimed composition proof will check all three execution paths through actual native observation and unchanged-map transitions, verify physical routes against their respective oracle, check logical demand and planner agreement, and verify weights and numerical outputs. Existing adversarial dispatcher checks will be retained. Structural or numerical failure will halt evaluation until a documented repair and new source freeze. Pilot timing will be descriptive and will not select implementations, thresholds, calendars or eligibility.

Final execution will first measure eight saturated native-only calibrations, one per payload-state/batch combination. Capacity C will be 256 divided by elapsed time from the first command start to final completion, including intervening coordinator work. Both treatments will use the same native calibration to define nominal load rho=0.5 or 0.85 and horizon H=256/(rho C). Calibration and evaluation must share the held allocation. The deterministic final manifest will be saved after calibration and before any final treatment run.

We will use eight new calendar blocks, combo-fresh-01 through combo-fresh-08, with seeds 2026110911 through 2026110918. Their payload-state assignment will be the ordered product of opt-pilot-01/02 and domain0/1, repeated twice. The captured payload sequence is reused; only the arrival calendars are new. Spread and clustered calendars will retain the frozen normalized gamma method, with shapes 4 and 0.25, 256 archived positive binary64 gap weights, exact-rational scaling and nearest-even nanosecond rounding. Releases rounded to the horizon will be capped at H−1 ns; no job will be omitted. These finite, normalized synthetic calendars are not unconditioned Poisson arrivals or naturally observed production traces. The same dimensionless gap draw will be scaled across loads and batches within each block.

The final matrix will contain eight blocks, two batches, two loads, two arrival shapes, three arms and two technical repetitions: 384 trials, 128 three-arm matched cases. Group order will use seed 471431. For each load, we will enumerate block IDs, batches and patterns in matrix order, shuffle the 32 case tuples with seed 471431+100+load_index, and assign the three cyclic rotations of [native,shortcut,combined] by (case_index+load_index) modulo three. Repetition 1 will reverse repetition 0. The order map will be archived in the manifest. Pairwise precedence is exactly balanced within each case; middle-arm position rotates across cases and is only nearly balanced because 32 is not divisible by three. We will report actual position counts and will not call this exact position balance.

The primary endpoint will be the combined/shortcut ratio of high-load arithmetic mean response within a trial. We will average the paired log ratios over technical repetitions, then equally over the four batch/shape cells within each calendar block, then equally over eight blocks. Practical success requires the upper bound of a two-sided 95% interval to be strictly below 0.95, establishing at least a five-percent incremental gain at that uncertainty level.

Three regression guards will compare combined against shortcut: low-load mean response, high-load trial response p95, and high-load mean response at batch 128. The latter prevents the known small-batch risk from being hidden by the larger batch. Each guard will require a one-sided (1−0.05/3)=98.333333% upper ratio bound at most 1.02. This Bonferroni family concerns those three aggregate guards, not all individual cells or comparisons against native. A crossing bound will mean the two-percent margin was not established; it will not automatically prove regression.

Only after the primary and all three guards pass will combined/native high-load mean response be tested as a hierarchical secondary endpoint, with a two-sided 95% interval and upper bound strictly below 0.95. Otherwise only its descriptive point estimate will be reported, without a confirmatory interval or pass flag; native-baseline gains cannot establish that adding the dispatcher is useful. Shortcut/native will always be descriptive. Every batch/load/shape cell will be reported for all three contrasts without extra cell-specific acceptance tests. Lower imbalance, reduced padding, zero movement bytes and shorter mean service will not substitute for response or completed elapsed measurements. Fixed offered load also prevents relabeling a response percentage as a throughput percentage.

All intervals will resample two entire calendar blocks with replacement within each of four fixed payload strata, using 100,000 PCG64 draws and seed 471432. Jobs and technical repetitions are nested observations. The design has only eight calendar blocks and 81 distinct stratum-count outcomes; a large bootstrap count does not create additional independent evidence. Inference is conditional on the reused payloads, native calibration, this allocation and the stated calendar generator. No experiment will be rerun selectively for a favorable result. All attempts, partial records and corrections will be retained; an incomplete matrix prevents a complete-study claim.

Raw records will retain immutable arrivals, coordinator and per-rank clocks, logical routes, both reconstructed physical assignments, arm-specific physical observation windows, map/weight hashes, actual mapper call counts, no-op agreement/skip counters, native decision logs, numerical checks and normal completion. GPU route checks will cover all 48 unique inputs; full-batch/native and sampled-FP32 numerical checks will occur outside timing. Independent CPU programs will reconstruct routing, calendars, state/order invariants and statistics from the archive. Computational reanalysis will not be described as an independent GPU rerun. Previous studies and their conclusions will remain unchanged.
