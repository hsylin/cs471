# ShareGPT input provenance

ShareGPT supplies authentic released conversation text, but this particular release already filters and splits the original sessions. The pinned vLLM benchmark documentation names this exact asset; its dataset card declares Apache-2.0 and describes language, repetition and content filtering plus conversation splitting around 2,048 upstream tokens. Preserving released messages therefore avoids a new 512-token cutoff without undoing the source's earlier processing. [Pinned vLLM dataset table](https://github.com/vllm-project/vllm/blob/db9527a46873454610df6dbedf79a36d6bf1a7f6/docs/benchmarking/cli.md#dataset-overview) · [Pinned dataset card](https://huggingface.co/datasets/anon8231489123/ShareGPT_Vicuna_unfiltered/blob/192ab2185289094fc556ec8ce5ce1e8e587154ca/README.md)

Our converter retains the complete released prefix through its last user message, including authentic assistant history, and excludes the following recorded answer. Assistant-initial segments remain explicitly marked. Source IDs append a numeric starting-message index; the pinned splitter establishes this rule, so `group_id` removes that suffix and the cohort builder must retain at most one segment per original conversation across pilot and final. [Upstream split_sample](https://huggingface.co/datasets/anon8231489123/ShareGPT_Vicuna_unfiltered/blob/192ab2185289094fc556ec8ce5ce1e8e587154ca/split_long_conversation.py#L23)

The acquired 672,837,942-byte asset matches its published LFS SHA256. There are 94,145 released rows and 92,562 executable text candidates before model tokenization/context filtering; excluded malformed or empty rows are counted in [the preparation record](sharegpt-preparation.json). Raw and candidate text remain outside Git. [The source manifest](sharegpt-source.json) records revision, checksums, license evidence and limitations. These are preparation counts, not inference measurements.
