**Estimand and uncertainty**

For each cell and seed, the analyzer averages the two log runtime ratios, log(T_B4/T_reference). The reported cell ratio exponentiates the mean of those ten seed values. Regime aggregates average the declared variant/batch cells within each seed before resampling entire seeds. This preserves pairing and avoids treating repeated runs or steps as independent observations. The burst primary endpoint weights its two phases and two batch sizes equally; it is not a production-traffic-weighted average or a requirement that every burst cell improve.

The analyzer draws 10,000 bootstrap samples of ten complete seed blocks and uses the 2.5th and 97.5th percentiles. The intervals are marginal, approximate percentile-bootstrap intervals. Ten clusters provide limited tail calibration; 10,000 resamples do not increase the number of independent workloads. The intervals describe this frozen hardware/configuration and trace-generating process, with measurement noise from this run campaign. Random method order within each paired block reduces order bias, but sequential seed blocks on one machine do not prove independence of thermal, clock, or system noise across time.

Overall success requires both burst aggregates, B4/B3 and B4/B5, to have their upper interval bound at most 0.95, and stable B4/B3 to remain within 1.02 both in aggregate and at each batch size. Requiring all five conditions is an intersection claim; simultaneous coverage of every displayed interval is neither provided nor needed to describe that predeclared conjunction. The many secondary cell/aggregate intervals must not be presented as a simultaneous 95% confidence band or searched for an additional confirmatory headline. The two-sided 95% upper endpoint is more stringent than a one-sided 95% upper bound.

The implementation of `fail` means that a required interval lies entirely above its specified margin. It does not necessarily mean B4 is slower than its reference: a confidently positive improvement smaller than 5% still fails the useful-gain requirement. An interval spanning a margin is inconclusive. Missing or invalid required runs prevent an overall verdict; the frozen design must not be shortened after inspecting results.

**Causal and external limits**

B4 versus B3 estimates the total effect of the additional stability predicate and its necessary observation/processing costs under the implemented synchronous path. Different subsequent maps and proposals are consequences of the policies, not a confound requiring identical later candidates. B5 tests one pilot-selected cooldown, not every possible frequency-control policy. SKIP preserves the common starting map and replica budget; it is not an optimized static-placement oracle.

The implementation uses real pretrained Qwen expert weights, synthetic Gaussian activations and controlled top-k routes, replicated inputs, and an output all-reduce. It establishes layer-harness behavior on two H100 GPUs. It does not establish full-model serving, production hotspot prevalence, dynamic request scheduling, asynchronous EPLB, or all-to-all transport performance. The primary endpoint includes the harness's shared instrumentation. Per-step event timings and component timers remain explanatory and must not replace the completed wall-clock endpoint.

The eight H=128 branch pairs are selected states from one seed and one scheduled decision index, across four regimes and two batches. They can establish the local consequence of admitting a particular candidate from an identical state. Four allow-first and four skip-first executions reduce systematic order bias but do not supply independent repeated measurements of each local effect. Planning and prefix execution are excluded, and no subsequent rebalancing occurs. Thus a branch payback is not the complete online policy benefit, and a TV-versus-payback scatter does not establish population selector accuracy or a causal effect of TV itself.
